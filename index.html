About me

Origin story

I'm Jebinmone, and I'm in the first year of B.Tech Information Technology. I started out curious about how the apps on my phone actually work, and that curiosity turned into wanting to build one myself.

Right now I'm at the stage where every small win feels big: a page that finally loads, a bug that finally makes sense. I'd rather learn slowly and understand it than copy something I can't explain.

What I like most is making something with my own hands, and then showing it to a friend and watching them use it.

The short version

I learn by building.

Read a little, make something, break it, fix it. Repeat until it's mine.
